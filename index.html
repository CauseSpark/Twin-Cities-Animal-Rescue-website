Our Mission

Twin Cities Animal Rescue rescues, rehabilitates, and rehomes abandoned and surrendered animals across the metro area. We believe no animal should wait alone for a second chance, and that the people who step forward to help deserve real support along the way. Our Impact This Year

  • 412 animals placed into permanent adoptive homes
  • 168 active foster families across seven counties
  • 1,940 volunteer hours contributed by community members
  • 96% of intakes placed within 60 days
